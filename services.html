Understanding Insurance Terms

Premium: The amount you pay for an insurance policy.

Deductible: The amount you may pay before certain covered expenses are paid by the insurance company.

Coverage Limit: The maximum amount an insurance policy may pay for a covered loss.

Policy Period: The period during which your insurance policy is active.

Always review your policy carefully to understand your coverage, exclusions, deductibles, and limits.
